Teaching tips

Sit together and let your child do the tapping. Point to each letter as the teacher says it. Red tiles are vowels, blue tiles are consonants, and wide tiles are letter teams like sh and ee. One or two islands a day is plenty; replaying an island gives new words.

Talk Time: your child's recordings stay on this device and are never saved or uploaded. To check words, the browser's own speech service listens (Google in Chrome, Apple in Safari). Turn the switch off to check by ear instead.
